The on-device answer

dinnerDecider turns your kitchen into a structured inventory, then into recipes you can cook right now.

Gemma 4 runs entirely on the phone. No server, no account, no round trip.

Fridge photos are intimate: our own test shots have medications, supplements, and alcohol next to the food. With dinnerDecider none of it ever leaves the phone. It works in airplane mode.

The hard part

Fitting Gemma 4 into 8 GB of phone.

An 8 GB iPhone gives roughly 4 GB of usable wired GPU memory. Fitting a 3.5 GB model plus vision projector plus KV cache under that ceiling took four moves:

  • Pick the UD-IQ3_XXS quant (3.46 GiB), smaller and faster than the alternatives
  • Shrink the context window to 1024 tokens
  • Move the vision projector onto the CPU, off the wired GPU budget
  • A lifecycle state machine so iOS memory pressure cannot unload the model mid-scan
Shipped six TestFlight builds today iterating on exactly this.

What’s next

A local LoRA fine-tune harness is ready, with a 1,288-example dataset: 75 verified real crops plus synthetic data from Open Food Facts. It drops into the app as a single GGUF file swap, no code change. That is the next lever on recall.
