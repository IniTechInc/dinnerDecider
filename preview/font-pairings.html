Why these five: they cover the useful spectrum for this app: native-neutral (1), warm editorial (2), soft friendly (3), classic cookbook (4), contemporary (5), and all are practical on iOS. One honest caution as your UX advisor: custom fonts on iOS cost a little Dynamic Type and accessibility polish compared to option 1, and with a hackathon deadline the safest play is option 1 for the whole UI, or a hybrid: custom display font for big headings and recipe names only, SF Pro for everything else. Options 2 and 4 work especially well as that hybrid.
